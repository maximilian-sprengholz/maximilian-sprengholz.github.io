# Maximilian Sprengholz

<p class="posthl-italic">(he/him)</p>

I am a data analyst at the [Bundesverband Deutscher Stiftungen](https://www.stiftungen.org), where I collect, analyze and communicate data to support foundations in their work for the common good. I hold a PhD in Sociology from Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, which I completed in 2025. There, I conducted quantitative research on labor market inequalities at the intersection of nativity and gender. Prior to that, I worked as a graphic designer.
